Ground Realities & Challenges

Why the Current System Fails Citizens & Government

1

Lead Seal & Paper Forgery

Physical lead wire seals are easily clamped, duplicated, or cut. Paper Form-VIII certificates are forged with color photocopies without any digital audit trail.

2

"Table Inspections"

Corrupt or overworked inspectors sign verification papers from their offices without physically visiting the shop or weighbridge to test calibration standards.

3

Zero Consumer Transparency

When an Indian consumer buys groceries or a truck rolls onto a highway weighbridge, they have NO instant way to check if the scale is legally calibrated or rigged.

Our Innovation Architecture

The 4 Anti-Fraud Pillars of DISHA AI

📍

1. GPS Geofence Anti-Ghost Guard

Inspector's device must be within 150m of registered shop GPS. App locks stamping if officer is away.

🔐

2. HMAC-SHA256 Signed Seals

Every certificate and QR code carries a cryptographic signature that instantly flags forged or modified records.

📡

3. Direct IoT Load-Cell Telemetry

Weight readings stream directly via RS-232/BLE into the verification checklist, eliminating manual data tampering.

📱

4. Citizen Phone Camera Verification

No app download needed. Pointing any smartphone camera at the scale sticker reveals the live Green Trust Badge.
